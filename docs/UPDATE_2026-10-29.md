# Update – 29 Oct 2026

What changed
- Title: **Prototype Cohort Harmoniser**; prototype disclaimer on every page and in the footer. No funding-scheme references.
- **TESS**: rebuilt from the revised instrument overview (150 rows × 11 columns; 683 of 1,490 time-point cells filled). Waves now aligned (T1 4y → T10 22y), so TESS appears on Two clocks. Added the IGDI (T4–T10), the clinical IGD interview (T4–T8), social media, FoMO, cyberbullying, parental social media/gaming, 7-day app time-use screenshots (T7–T10) and retrospective objective app records matched per wave (study-lead confirmation). Norwegian notes removed.
- **Understanding Society**: rebuilt from the targeted harmoniser dictionary (1,433 item × construct rows, 958 stems) across UKHLS, BHPS, Innovation Panel, COVID-19 Study, PEACH, Wellbeing App and Twitter linkage; all three domains are now populated.
- **ADII**: Australian Digital Inclusion Index added to the invisible room (panel with national, state, remoteness and subgroup values), as Shading options on Two clocks, as a column in the room table, and under every Australian item in Item compare.
- **LSAC** (second pass, same day): full Release 10.1 dictionary loaded (77,039 rows; B and K cohorts, W1–W10, parent/teacher/study-child informants), replacing the Wave-1 extract.
- **GUiNZ** (second pass): full dictionary loaded (43 tabs, 16,035 variables, antenatal → 15y), replacing the crosswalk. Problematic screen-use items TU114–TU119 (12y), SDQ, PROMIS, KIDSCREEN, SWEMWBS-worded WBE1–7 (15y) and the Cantril ladder are now item-level.
- **Clean-up**: keyword false positives removed by rule after hand checking (HILDA, MCS, LSAC, GUI, GUiNZ), all listed in `data/removed_false_positives.csv`. KIDSCREEN items (caught by the word 'screen') moved from screen time to life satisfaction.
- New anchors: SWEMWBS (UKHLS ↔ GUiNZ, to verify) and IGD criteria (TESS ↔ ABCD); SDQ, Rosenberg, PBI, MCS 7-item and life-satisfaction anchors updated.
